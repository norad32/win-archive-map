About Win Archive Map

What is this?

Win Archive Map is an interactive way to explore historical images from the Bildarchiv Winterthur in their geographic context.

Browse places and streets, compare historic maps and aerial imagery, and discover related articles from the Winterthur-Glossar.

How do I use it?

  • Search words in a title, or filter by year, district, neighbourhood or street.
  • Choose Digital Map or Aerial Image, then use the year slider to browse available historic editions.
  • Click a marker to see its archive record. Marker size indicates the number of records at a location, the exact number of entries is available by tooltip.
  • View in Bildarchiv searches the exact entry in Bildarchive. Search in Bildarchiv searches the Bildarchiv by metadata and may reveal similar images.
  • Zoom to results fits the current results to the screen.
  • Clear Filters resets the filters.
  • Right-click the map to copy coordinates.

The coordinates of this landmark are wrong

Open the marker and select Report incorrect metadata in the record details. This opens a pre-filled GitHub issue. Describe the problem and, if possible, include the correct location or coordinates (right-click the map to copy them). The same link can also be used to report an incorrect title, date or address.

Who is this cute lion?

Why its Geoleo the friendly, starburst lion from Winterthur. He sits on the selected marker to help you spot the location you’re exploring.

Data Source

Image metadata (address, year, title and district) is sourced from the Bildarchiv Winterthur .

District and neighbourhood boundaries are sourced from Stadtkreise und Quartiere Zürich und Winterthur (OGD), provided by Geoinformation, Amt für Raumentwicklung, Kanton Zürich, licensed under CC0.
